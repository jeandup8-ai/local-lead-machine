Privacy notice

Plain version: we only collect what you choose to give us, we use it to run your audit and reply to you, and we don't sell it.

Who we are

Local Lead Machine (“we”) runs this website. You can reach us via the contact page.

What we collect

  • Audit and enquiry forms: the details you type in — such as your name, business name, phone, WhatsApp number, email, city, website and Google profile link — and your audit result.
  • Payments: handled by PayFast on its own secure pages. We receive confirmation of payment, not your card details.
  • Website checks: when you run an audit we fetch the public web page you entered to check it. We don't log in to anything or access private data.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may record standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to limit abuse.

We don't ask for ID numbers, banking details or other sensitive personal information on this site. Please don't send them through our forms.

How we use it

  • To produce and explain your audit.
  • To reply to your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To provide the service if you become a customer.

We don't sell your information and we don't add you to bulk marketing lists. If we contact a business about its public online presence, we do so individually and stop if asked.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored by our website host (Netlify, Inc.) and sent to our email. Those providers may store data outside South Africa.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become customers are deleted within 12 months. Customer records are kept as long as needed for the service and any legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — just contact us. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. South Africans can also contact the Information Regulator if they have concerns about how their personal information is handled.
