The leaks

Where are you losing customers?

These are the eight leaks we check for. Most local businesses have at least three.

Leak

No clear website

Customers who want more than a Google listing find nothing — so they pick the electrician who does have a proper page.

Leak

No WhatsApp button

Lots of South Africans would rather WhatsApp than call. If they can't tap to chat, many simply move on.

Leak

Unclear services

“Electrical work” doesn't tell someone with a tripping DB board that you're the right person.

Leak

No service areas

If customers can't see you work in their suburb, they assume you don't.

Leak

Poor mobile experience

Pinching, zooming and tiny buttons on a phone = closed tab.

Leak

No strong quote button

People ready to buy are looking for “Get a quote”. If it's hidden, the enquiry goes elsewhere.

Leak

Weak trust signals

No reviews, no photos, no business name up top. Customers can't tell if you're legit.

Leak

Outdated information

Old numbers, old hours, a 2017 footer. It makes people wonder if you're still trading.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly do you build?

A fast, mobile-first page for your business with tap-to-call, a WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message, your services, the areas you cover, a quote request form, a trust section that links to your real Google reviews, FAQs and basic local SEO setup. We also give you practical tips for your Google Business Profile.

Do I need an existing website?

No. Many of the businesses we help don't have one. If you do, we can replace it or build a focused page alongside it.

Do I need to change my Google Business Profile?

Usually just small updates — like adding your new page link, checking your services and service areas. We'll tell you exactly what to change. You stay in control of your profile.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. What we fix is the part you control: making it quick and easy for people who already find you to call, WhatsApp or ask for a quote.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Rankings depend on Google, your competition, your reviews and your area. We set up solid local SEO foundations, but we won't promise positions.

Can customers WhatsApp me?

Yes. A WhatsApp button opens a chat with your number and a ready-made message, so customers only need to tap send.

Can customers call me directly?

Yes. Your number becomes a one-tap “Call now” button on phones. Calls come straight to you — we're not a middleman.

How quickly can you launch?

Usually within 24 hours of receiving your business details, logo/photos and payment. If something on our side delays it, we'll tell you upfront.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You send us your details on WhatsApp. We do the setup and walk you through anything you need to do on your side.

Is there a monthly contract?

No. The setup is once-off. Care & Optimisation (R399/month) is optional and you can cancel anytime.

What happens after the introductory offer?

Once the first three founding customers are done, the setup price goes to the normal rate (from R2,490). Founding customers keep what they paid for.
