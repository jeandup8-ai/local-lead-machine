Fill these in once — every script updates. Only use issues you've actually verified.

First contact

Hi [Name], I found [Business] while looking for local electricians. I noticed a few things in your online customer journey that may be making it harder for people to contact you. I'm doing a small launch for local businesses and offering a free 5-minute audit. No obligation. Would you like me to send it?

Follow-up (2–3 days later)

Hi [Name], just following up on this. I spotted [verified issue]. It's a small fix, but it's exactly the sort of thing I look for when helping local businesses turn searches into enquiries.

Sending the audit link

Here's the free audit — it checks your website and shows what's working and what might be costing you calls: [Audit link]
Happy to talk you through it.

After the audit

Hi [Name], I've finished the audit. There are [X] areas I'd improve, with the biggest being [verified issue]. I can fix the conversion side for [Price] during our launch period. No long contract.

Collecting details (after payment)

Great — to build your page I need:
1. Business name + the number and WhatsApp customers should use
2. The services you actually do
3. The suburbs/areas you cover
4. Your logo and 3–6 photos of real work (if you have them)

Send them here whenever you're ready.

Asking for a testimonial (after launch)

Hi [Name], glad the page is live. If you're happy with how it went, would you mind writing one or two honest sentences about working with me? Only if you're genuinely happy — no pressure.

If they say no

No problem at all, thanks for letting me know. If anything changes, you know where to find me. All the best with the business.

Rules that keep this honest

  • Message people one at a time, from your own number. No bulk blasting.
  • Only mention issues you've checked yourself.
  • If someone says no or asks you to stop, stop — and mark them LOST in the tracker.
  • Never claim results, rankings or reviews you can't back up.
